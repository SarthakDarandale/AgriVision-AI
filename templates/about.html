About AgriVision AI

AgriVision AI is an AI-powered Smart Agriculture Assistant designed to help farmers make better decisions using Machine Learning, weather information and crop intelligence.

🌱 What is AgriVision AI?

AgriVision AI is a web-based smart agriculture platform developed to provide useful agricultural information and intelligent recommendations.

The system uses Machine Learning to recommend a suitable crop based on soil nutrients and weather conditions. It also provides plant disease detection, live weather information and detailed information about crops grown in Maharashtra.

The main goal of the project is to demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence and web technologies can be combined to support smarter farming decisions.
